# Waxline — MODDING

Waxline is data first. A mod changes one of three things, and a wall says whether the game still plays right after it:

| What | Where | The wall that reads it |
|---|---|---|
| the park, the tricks, the gaps, the physics, the look, the sound | `build/js/config.js` — plain data, one table each | `sh tests/run_all.sh` (Node, no browser) |
| the character (Juno): his body, clothes, hair, face | `sources/juno/` — Blender scripts run headless, a CC0 base body | `sources/juno/juno_walls.mjs`, `mirror.py`, `bind_check.mjs` |
| how anything moves | `build/js/formulas/` (every spring, the fixed step, the contact plane, the fall) and `PHYSICS` / `LEGS` | `tests/motion.mjs`, `tests/motion_sweep.mjs`, `tests/surfaces.mjs` |

Every number is in a file; nothing is generated by a model at run time. Everything below was measured on the tree as it stands (2026-10-16).

## 1. The files

```
waxline/
  DESIGN.md      the design: the contract, the controls, the physics, the park, the art, every bound and where it came from
  CLEANROOM.md   every source the game was made from (real skating: papers, footage, measurements) — never another game's code, art or motion
  MODDING.md     this
  build/                     what ships (uploaded as it is)
    index.html · port.js · mosa-port.js (the platform's kit, never edited) · CREDITS.md · LICENSE · licenses/
    js/config.js             THE MOD FILE: PARK · GAPS · LEGS · PHYSICS · INPUT · ASSISTS · TRICKS · TREES · RUN · SCORING · CAMERA · LOOK ·
                             SKATERS · BOARD · SOUND (GOALS are in js/game/run.js)
    js/main.js               boot, the fixed-step loop, the game's modes
    js/formulas/             the standard formulas, one module each, every caller on them:
                               step.js (the fixed step and the drawn frame between two steps) · follow.js (every spring) ·
                               contact.js (the plane through four wheels) · jump.js (height and time, the exact fall) · stroke.js (the push)
    js/sim/                  park.js (the data → the collision mesh, the grind lines, the lips, the park's own checks) · world.js · bvh.js ·
                             body.js (the board and the rider: the state machine) · score.js · vec.js
    js/input/                touch.js · zones.js (who owns a touch: the look band or a thumb) · reader.js (the flick grader)
    js/view/                 what is drawn: parkview (the park's meshes) · surfaces (their materials) · trees · stage (light, sky) ·
                             skater (the rig, the IK, the pose) · figure (the drawn state) · sculpt + cloth (Juno as sculpted in code, to V13) ·
                             board · camera · sightline (the camera's rule) · fall · hud · style
    js/audio/audio.js        every sound, synthesized (no audio files)
    js/game/run.js           the two-minute run and its goals
  sources/juno/              the character's pipeline (§5) — its outputs ship, its scripts do not
  tests/                     the walls: *.mjs in Node; drive/*.mjs in a browser on the GPU; lib/ their shared readers; plants.mjs (§6)
```

The sim imports nothing from `view/`: the Node walls, the bot and the multiplayer drive the same code the page runs.

## 2. The data, table by table (`build/js/config.js`)

Units are metres, seconds, degrees (where a field says so) and the park's own coordinates: x east, z north, y up, the plaza's south-west
corner at (0, 0), the park 62 × 44 m.

**`PARK`** — `size`; `ground` (each level: `rect [x0, z0, x1, z1]`, its height `y`, its `surface`); `elements` (below); `spawn`; `lamps`;
`nodes` (where a bail puts you back: x, y, z, heading). Every element is one row:

| `type` | its fields |
|---|---|
| `quarter` | `coping [[x, z], [x, z]]` (the lip's line) · `face [dx, dz]` (the riding side, from the lip) · `base` (the floor's height) · `h` · `r` (the transition's radius) · `vStar` (its roll-in speed, m/s: below it a board ROLLS over the coping, at and over it DROPS IN; default 0 — never set it above what `node tests/vstar.mjs <id>` measures for the lip: that wall fails) |
| `spine` | `coping` · `base` · `h` · `r` — ridden both ways, square to its coping |
| `bowl` | `rect` · `corner` (the rounded corners' radius) · `depth` · `r` — cuts its hole in the ground · `vStar` (as a quarter's) |
| `ledge` | `rect`, or `line [[x, z], [x, z]]` + `width` (a slanted ledge) · `h` · `base` (the level it stands on) · `top`: `'granite'` (with `wax: true`, its top and edges waxed: less friction), `'curb'`, else slab |
| `rail` | `pts [[x, y, z], …]` (y: the rail's own height) · `posts: true` |
| `box` | `base [x0, z0, x1, z1]` (its foot) · `top [x0, z0, x1, z1]` (its deck) · `h` |
| `stairs` | `rect` · `top` (the upper level's y) · `risers` · `rise` · `tread` |
| `stairLedge` | `rect` · `profile [[z, y], …]` |
| `bank` | `rect` · `from` · `to` (the heights at its two ends) |
| `planter` · `bench` | `rect` · `h` |

Every element with a footprint gets its grind lines, its lips and its collision from `js/sim/park.js` — add a row and it is drawn, ridden
and ground. **The park's own checks** name a row that leaves the park or lies on another (by more than a centimetre, each footprint as its
real polygon on the ground) in the console (`[waxline] the park says: …`) and `tests/park.mjs` fails on it. They never refuse a row: a mod
may put a ledge over another on purpose, and it is built.

**`GAPS`** — each `{ name, value, kind, … }`: `air` (from a rect to a rect, landing at `toY`) · `grind` (`rail` the element's id; `full: true`
end to end, or `length` metres) · `clear` (`over` an element) · `transfer` (`fromRail` → a rect) · `transfer2` (`a` → `b`, two faces) ·
`manual` (`region`, `length`) · `vert` (`quarter`, `height` above its lip).

**`TRICKS`** — `flips`: `{ name, base, stroke, flip, shove }`. The stroke is the shape the right thumb draws after the load (0° = up the
screen, −90° = left, +90° = right): `{ kind: 'line', dir }` · `{ kind: 'curve', from, to }` · `{ kind: 'loop', from, turn }`. `flip` is
turns about the board's long axis (−1: a kickflip's one turn), `shove` turns about the vertical (−0.5: a backside 180). `doubles` (a long
stroke at speed grade 5), `specials` (golden meter only), `grabs` (`dir` held), `grinds` and `manuals` (`[base, per second]`), `reverts`,
`flipTime` (seconds a turn takes at grades 1, 3, 5). Trick names are real skating names — they are free; no brand is.

**`SCORING`** — the repeat factors, the stance bonuses, the spin factor, the grade, the bank time, the special meter (DESIGN §5.2; the
worked example 4,875 is `tests/combo.mjs`). **`RUN`** — the run's `seconds` and the five wax bars (`wax`, x y z each).

**`PHYSICS`** — the fixed `step` (1/120 s) and everything the board and rider obey: gravity on the ground and in the air, the pop's height
by flick grade, the push (`push`: its gain, its top speed, its peak at 1.3 g, the foot's reach and swing), rolling resistance per surface
(`roll`), the carve, the brake, the spin, the wheels' probes, the grind's catch (`grind`), friction per surface (`friction`), the landing
windows (`land`), manuals, lips, reverts, vert airs, the roll-in, the bail, balance. **`LEGS`** — the body on its legs: the hips' spring
(`hz` 4, `zeta` 1), the legs' reach (`sink`, `rise`, `sway`), the body's attitude (`tilt`, `tiltHz`, `tiltAcc`). Every line says its
source (CLEANROOM.md) — change a number and the walls (§4) say what it did.

**`INPUT`** — the look band's share of the screen, the thumbs' dead zone and full throw, the flick reader's thresholds. **`ASSISTS`** — the
three presets (`cruise`, the default · `classic` · `raw`): catch, grind, balance, pump, vert, spin, 0–100.

**`CAMERA`** (filmer, high, bail) · **`LOOK`** (the sun, the sky, the fill, the painted look, the `palette` of every park material) ·
**`TREES`** (x, z) · **`SKATERS`** (Juno's colours: skin, jacket, tee, pants, beanie, hair, shoes, soles, laces, the stitching) · **`BOARD`**
(a 31.75 × 8 in deck: its length, width, wheelbase, kick, concave, plies, grip, the bottom graphic's colours and word) · **`SOUND`** (every
sound synthesized: each surface's roll in three layers, the joints' spacing, the clack, the pop, the grind's partials).

## 3. A worked example: a new ledge on the plaza

**1. The row** — in `PARK.elements`:

```js
{ id: 'plazaLedge', type: 'ledge', name: 'Plaza Ledge', rect: [27, 7.0, 33, 7.5], h: 0.38, top: 'granite', wax: true },
```

**2. The park's own checks** — `node tests/park.mjs`:

```
built in 30 ms · 3166 collision triangles · 3264 drawn · 35 grindable lines · 6 lips
✔ the park's own checks: every element inside the park, no two on one spot (22 elements)
PARK: GREEN
```

The first place tried for this ledge was `rect: [14, 12.0, 20, 12.5]` — inside the Basin (x 2–16, z 3–17). The checks say so:

```
✖ the park's own checks: every element inside the park, no two on one spot — The Basin and Plaza Ledge on one spot (overlapping by 50 cm)
```

**3. A run over it** — in `tests/surfaces.mjs`, beside the Main Ledge's runs (`RUNS.push([name, start, the input, frames])`): start 0.3 m
off the ledge's south edge going east, ollie 0.3 m past its start:

```js
RUNS.push(['Plaza Ledge, an ollie onto it going east at 4.5 m/s', { at: [26, 0, 6.7], heading: 90, speed: 4.5 }, ollieWhen((sk) => sk.p[0] >= 27.3), 150]);
```

**4. Read it** — `node tests/surfaces.mjs "Plaza Ledge"` runs that run alone:

```
  · Plaza Ledge, an ollie onto it going east at 4.5 m/s [roll air grind; pop grindStart grindEnd land]: jump 0.8 cm (ℹ air 0.3) ·
    hips ground 4, impulse 104, ℹ air 44 m/s² · gap sink 5.6 rise 1.8 sway 3.6 cm · straight 0.896 · pins 0.42 cm RMS, 0.6 through impulses ·
    deck over its contacts 0°/s · joints 1128°/s
— ROLLED IN, NOT FLOWN: not read — no run of its kind in this filter (Plaza Ledge)
…
SURFACES: GREEN · 1 runs · 4 not read (filtered)
```

It rolled, popped, caught the ledge (`grindStart`), ground to its end and landed. Every bound held: the deck's move on the ground 0.8 cm
(≤ 1), the hips 4 m/s² rolling (≤ 60) and 104 through the pop and the catch (≤ 150), the legs within reach, the feet pinned. A gate that
needs another kind of run (a roll-in, a lip air) is **not read** in a filtered run, and says so; unfiltered, it must pass.

**5. The whole suite** — `sh tests/run_all.sh` → `ALL NODE TESTS: GREEN`. Then the device round (§4) before anyone plays it.

## 4. The walls

- **`sh tests/run_all.sh`** — every Node wall, one line each: parse · formulas (the standard formulas' own truths) · hash · hard_gates · park ·
  sculpt · face · motion · motion_sweep · legs_step · surfaces (every run on every preset; a run with `reported: '<finding>'` is ridden, said
  and never gated) · vstar (every lip's roll-in speed, measured) · slide (a stop at a face keeps its step) · catch_from_above (a rail is met
  from above) · fullscreen (the full-screen control, offered only where it can act) · hands (at the thighs' sides; the balance arms out) · reader (the flick grader: 11 tricks × 300 synthetic thumbs at each sample rate and wobble) · zones (looking never
  eats a flick) · tricks_reachable (every trick, by its own stroke, through the real reader into the real sim, lands and is named) · combo ·
  handling · determinism (three 10-minute sessions, bit for bit) · run_goals · camera_view · camera_moving · `plants.mjs check`.
- **`sh tests/round.sh <n>`** — the device round: the page in a real browser on the GPU, upright and turned (the census, the states, the
  sun, the floor, the hashes, the kit, the cloth, the face, the HUD, even motion, frame times, the bots in WebKit and Chromium, the
  full-screen control under the platform's frame and at the top of its own window, a turn mid-run, landscape and notched with the HUD read
  against the safe area, the phone's weight turned and back, the sound, the weight),
  then every plant. On a shared machine: one at a time (`bash ~/.mosa/heavy.sh run <name> -- sh tests/round.sh <n>`).
- **Where a bug starts** — the motion walls record the chain frame by frame: sim → board → pose → trunk → target → legs → ik → bones → mesh.
  Under a RED gate the wall prints the first stage that broke (`↳ …`): start reading there.

## 5. The character — Juno (`sources/juno/`)

The base body is Blender Studio's Human Base Meshes v1.4.1 (CC0 — `sources/juno/base/README.md` keeps its hashes and both dates). Only
the base body is someone else's; the clothes, the hair, the face's detail and every colour are made by these scripts. Every Blender call
goes through `blender.sh` (background, factory settings, a downloaded .blend's own scripts never run):

| Step | Script | What it makes |
|---|---|---|
| the skeleton | `node sources/juno/skeleton_out.mjs` | `skeleton.json`: the game's own bones (names, parents, heads, tails) — the rest pose the game's animation is written against |
| the fit | `sh blender.sh --python fit_base.py -- --bundle <the .blend> --skeleton skeleton.json --work work/` | `work/juno_fitted.blend`: the base posed onto the game's skeleton with the game's bone lengths, held symmetric |
| the clothes | `sh blender.sh work/juno_fitted.blend --python dress.py -- --skeleton skeleton.json --work work/` | `work/juno_dressed.blend`: the cargos, tee, jacket, collar, shoes, soles, beanie, hair; the skin cut away where they hide it |
| the export | `sh blender.sh work/juno_dressed.blend --python export_juno.py -- --skeleton skeleton.json --out work/juno_lod0.glb --lod 0` (and `--lod 1`) | the glTF on the game's bones: weights (four a vertex, summing to 1), the sky shade `_OCC`, each piece's material named for its part |
| the walls | `node juno_walls.mjs <GLTFLoader.js> [--plant open\|pinch]` · `sh blender.sh work/juno_dressed.blend --python mirror.py` · `node bind_check.mjs <glb> <GLTFLoader.js>` · `node face_check.mjs <GLTFLoader.js>` · `node deform_check.mjs <a --with-body 1 glb> <GLTFLoader.js> [--plant far]` | each piece's shape as designed (nothing pinched, each garment one closed piece of its own χ, the skin its five cut pieces), ≤ 30,000 triangles (LOD 0) and ≤ 12,000 (LOD 1); the face's mirror ≤ 0.1 mm; the weights on the game's push and ollie; the lids open and the two eyes alike, the pupil and the iris at every look the game gives; the flesh's volume at the elbows, knees and shoulders and nothing pushed into the body, over the game's own motion |
| a look | `bash ~/.mosa/heavy.sh run <name> -- sh blender.sh work/juno_dressed.blend --python render.py -- --out work/renders` | the whole figure from four sides, the face at 0.6 m from five |

Measured on 2026-10-16: LOD 0 28,994 triangles, LOD 1 11,794; every piece as designed on both (the tee χ −4, the jacket χ −2, the rest
χ 2, the skin open where it was cut); the face in front of the ears mirrors within 0.044 mm (the whole head, the ears with it, 0.19), the
eyes within 0.037 mm.

**What a mod may change, and what it may not:** a garment is one `grow()` or `sheet()` line in `dress.py` (its region, its ease, its part);
the colours are `SKATERS` in config. **The skeleton is the game's and never changes** — the bone lengths are what the IK and every motion wall
read, so a body fitted to anything else stops following the game's motion. In the game, V13 still draws the Juno sculpted in code
(`view/sculpt.js`, `view/cloth.js`); the Blender Juno replaces him in V14, on the same bones.

## 6. Plants — proving a wall can fail

`tests/plants.mjs` holds every defect the game once had, or one a wall exists to keep out: its edit, the wall that must catch it, and the
line that proves the catch is that defect. `node tests/plants.mjs check` (does every edit still apply?) runs in the Node suite; a round runs
them all (`plants.mjs run <dir>`). A new wall comes with its plant: the two park checks above are `park_two_on_one_spot` (the Basin ledge)
and `park_row_leaves` (a row past the fence).

## 7. The rules a mod keeps

- Nothing from another game: no code, no art, no motion, no sound. Motion comes from real skating, and its source goes in CLEANROOM.md.
- `build/mosa-port.js` (the platform's kit) and the vendored three.js are never edited.
- A new image, model or sound file is credited in `build/CREDITS.md` with its licence in `build/licenses/`.
